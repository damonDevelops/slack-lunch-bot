Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 May 2026

What we collect

When you install Lunchbot, we store your Slack workspace's bot token and your personal Slack user OAuth token. These are required to set your Slack status on your behalf.

We also receive the text you type after /lunch and send it to Anthropic's Claude API to determine an appropriate emoji and status text. We do not store this text.

How we use it

Your user token is used solely to call Slack's users.profile.set API to update your status. We do not use it for any other purpose.

Tokens are stored in AWS DynamoDB in the ap-southeast-2 (Sydney) region.

What we don't do

We do not sell, share, or otherwise distribute your data to any third party. We do not read your Slack messages. We do not store your lunch descriptions.

Third-party services

Anthropic Claude — lunch descriptions are sent to Claude to pick an emoji. Anthropic's privacy policy applies: anthropic.com/privacy.

AWS — tokens are stored in AWS DynamoDB. AWS's privacy policy applies: aws.amazon.com/privacy.

Deleting your data

To remove your data, uninstall Lunchbot from your Slack workspace or email us at damon.oneil2@hotmail.com and we will delete your tokens within 7 days.
