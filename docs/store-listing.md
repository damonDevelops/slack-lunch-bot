# Lunchbot — Slack App Store Listing

## App Name

Lunchbot

## Short Description

Set your Slack status to what you're eating, with the perfect emoji. Just type `/lunch` and let AI do the rest.

## Long Description

Lunchbot turns your lunch into your Slack status — automatically.

Just type `/lunch a spicy burrito 45m` and Lunchbot picks the most fitting emoji, sets your status text, and clears it when your time is up. No more hunting through the emoji picker or forgetting to update your status.

## How it works

- `/lunch [what you're eating] [optional duration]` — sets your status instantly
- `/lunch clear` — clears your status early
- Duration is optional — defaults to 30 minutes if you don't specify

Powered by Claude AI for emoji selection that actually makes sense.
