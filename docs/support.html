Commands

/lunch [what you're eating] [optional duration]
e.g. /lunch a spicy burrito 45m
/lunch clear  or  /lunch done
Clears your status immediately

Duration can be written as 30m, 1h, or 1.5h. If you leave it out, your status will clear after the default duration set by your workspace admin (usually 30 minutes).

First-time setup

After your workspace admin installs Lunchbot, each user needs to authorise it once before using /lunch. When you first run the command, Lunchbot will send you a link to do this — just click it and approve.

Common issues

"Before I can set your status, connect your account first" — click the authorise link in that message to connect your Slack account.

"Couldn't figure out that lunch" — try describing your food differently. The more specific, the better.

"Couldn't set your status" — try re-authorising via the link provided. If the issue persists, contact us below.
